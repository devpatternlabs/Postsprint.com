Account safety

Your accounts should stay yours.

Postsprint is designed around connecting supported platforms through their official authorization flows — not collecting social media passwords.

A clearer connection

Connect with permission. Stay in control.

Meta and Google provide authorization tools that allow services to request specific permissions without asking for your Facebook, Instagram or Google passwords.

Safe

How connections work

What to expect when connecting an account

1
Choose your platform.

Start a connection from the Postsprint connected accounts screen.

2
Review the provider’s permission screen.

Meta or Google presents the requested access. Only approve permissions you understand.

3
Return to your workspace.

The application shows the connection status after authorization finishes.

4
Keep your control.

Review or revoke authorization through provider settings; local disconnect availability depends on the implemented integration.

This page describes the intended authorization model, not a completed security audit or confirmed Meta/Google approval. Final public claims must be checked against the live implementation.
